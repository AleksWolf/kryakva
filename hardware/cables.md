# Servo bus cables

19 AMP 2.0-3P cables, pin to pin (1–1, 2–2, 3–3): 9 from the servo boxes (15 cm), the rest as listed in [bom.md](bom.md).
For your own cables: 24 AWG wire, insulation no thicker than 1.50 mm (for the A2007-T terminal), A2007H-3P housings.
