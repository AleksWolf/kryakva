# Print profiles

Three Bambu Studio projects. The parts are arranged on the plates and oriented for printing; quantities are per duck.

## Which profile to choose

- **Printer with two nozzles or mechanical nozzle change**: [microduck-x2d-0.4.3mf](microduck-x2d-0.4.3mf) and [microduck-x2d-0.6.3mf](microduck-x2d-0.6.3mf)
  (made for Bambu Lab X2D). You need both projects: 0.4 has the rigid parts, 0.6 has the soft parts and the roller wheels.
- **Other printers**: [microduck-p2s-0.4.3mf](microduck-p2s-0.4.3mf) (made for Bambu Lab P2S, one 0.4 nozzle). It has all the duck parts.

## What is in the projects

| File | Nozzle | Parts | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| [microduck-x2d-0.4.3mf](microduck-x2d-0.4.3mf) | 0.4 | all rigid parts, 40 pcs, 6 plates | PLA Basic; `zero clamp` — ASA-CF; support interface layer — PETG Basic |
| [microduck-x2d-0.6.3mf](microduck-x2d-0.6.3mf) | 0.6 | soles, `jaw_soft`, `soft_mouth_top`, 4 roller wheels (rim and tire print as one part), 8 pcs, 3 plates | TPU 85A (0.6 nozzle); wheel rim and supports — PLA Basic |
| [microduck-p2s-0.4.3mf](microduck-p2s-0.4.3mf) | 0.4 | all parts, 52 pcs, 6 plates; wheel rim and tire as separate parts, stretch the tire onto the rim | PLA Basic; `zero clamp` — ASA; soft parts and tires — TPU 90A (0.4 nozzle) |

Layer 0.2 mm on the 0.4 nozzle and 0.3 mm on the 0.6 nozzle. Supports are enabled on the parts that need them. Plate: Textured PEI.

For another printer, take the parts and their orientation from these projects and the settings from the table.
